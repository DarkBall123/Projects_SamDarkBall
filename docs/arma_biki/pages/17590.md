## Eden Editor: Toolbar

- Source: https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Eden_Editor:_Toolbar
- Revision: 336440
- Updated: 2023-01-04T15:21:42Z

The **Toolbar** is displayed at the top of the Workspace. It provided quick access to commonly used actions and tools.
File:3den toolbar.jpg
