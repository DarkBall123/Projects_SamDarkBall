## CSLA Iron Curtain Polanek

- Source: https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/CSLA_Iron_Curtain_Polanek
- Revision: 339470
- Updated: 2023-04-28T14:08:58Z

Polanek is the name of 25km2 sized map included in CSLA Iron Curtain - Operations expansion pack. The map is based on an area in southern Moravia in and around the town of [Ždánice](https://goo.gl/maps/vdQ2vKg1WUbP33dSA) to represent some more of the inland territory of Czechoslovakia.

While the map is freely accessible on Steam Workshop, the main cDLC is still required to run the map.
