## Turret Path

- Source: https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Turret_Path
- Revision: 329896
- Updated: 2022-08-08T09:33:51Z

A **Turret Path** is an Array of Numbers that determines a turret seat's "path", a turret being able to have a turret that has a turret etc.

Turrets' notion was introduced with {{GVI|arma1|1.00}}.

```mediawiki-table
{| class="align-right-col-1"
| [-1]
| means driver seat
|-
| [0]
| means first turret (usually gunner)
|-
| [1]
| means second turret
|-
| [0,0]
| means first turret's first turret
|-
| [0,1]
| means first turret's second turret
|-
| [1,0]
| means second turret's first turret, etc.
|}
```
