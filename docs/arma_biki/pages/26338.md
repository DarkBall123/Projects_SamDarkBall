## Virtual Reality

- Source: https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Virtual_Reality
- Revision: 364320
- Updated: 2024-05-06T09:38:47Z

thumb|left|A soldier in VR gear
thumb|right|VR ground square sizes
Virtual Reality is a virtual space similar to Render Worlds with no elements nor terrain relief in sight with the exception of a small, squared lake to the  North-East of the "place".

Both ground and skybox are grey and squared with white lines. A big square is 32x32m and contains 256 (16x16) small squares of 2x2m.

The centre of the editor terrain is X:4000 - Y:4000, which is in the middle of a big square; the terrain itself is infinite.

VR blocks glow in the dark (but terrain lines don't).
