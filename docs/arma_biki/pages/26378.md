### Altis

#### Atlantis

There is a myth that remnants of an ancient civilization lies underwater near the coast of Altis|Arma 3 loading screen
"Atlantis" can be found at **150068** ({{Link|https://www.youtube.com/watch?v{{=}}SUM-N_p3kbc&t{{=}}1m14s|source}})

#### Stonehenge

Altis also has its (nicer) Stonehenge with a decorative skull on the top, located in the North-East corner of **144061** at Cape Makrinos.

#### Rocky hideout

A hidden hideout (and what is supposedly its owner's skeleton) can be found at **080094** ({{Link|https://www.youtube.com/watch?v{{=}}SUM-N_p3kbc&t{{=}}2m13s|source}})

#### TRPASLIK

**TRPASLIK** is written with rocks on the bottom of the sea at **229061** ({{Link|https://www.youtube.com/watch?v{{=}}SUM-N_p3kbc&t{{=}}25s|source}})
[text= Trpaslik](link= https://forums.bohemia.net/profile/826110-trpaslik/) is Altis' Environment Designer ([REPORT IN](https://arma3.com/news/report-in-radim-vitek-petr-sedlacek-environment)).

### Stratis

#### Pirate hideout

According to legend, there is a hidden cave somewhere on the coast of Stratis, rumored to contain treasures hidden by the Arabian pirates.|Arma 3 loading screen
The mentioned **Pirate Cave** is located at **042073** ([https://www.youtube.com/watch?v{{=}}a03c1WLZVu8&t{{=}}27s how-to video])

#### V-22 Osprey wreck

The **V-22 Osprey** wreck found South of Stratis airport (coordinates **014047**) is a nod to {{arma2}}'s vehicle ({{Link|https://www.youtube.com/watch?v{{=}}a03c1WLZVu8&t{{=}}2m01s|video}})

### Malden 2035

#### Stonehenge

Same as in {{ofp}}, Malden 2035 has its own https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stonehenge, located in the North-West corner of **058101** coordinates.

thumb|Dedicated to Paul “Bushlurker” Pelosi (1960-2017)
#### The Bushlurker Monument

In the beginning, there was no clutter... Only bushes...
At **027074** lays a monument in tribute to [text= Paul “Bushlurker” Pelosi (1960-2017)](link= https://forums.bohemia.net/forums/topic/201520-paul-%E2%80%9Cbushlurker%E2%80%9D-pelosi-1960-2017-rest-in-peace/) who has worked on many community terrains, including Malden for CWR2. He passed away some months before Malden 2035's release.

[text= The statue](link= https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=843661230) itself was made by [text= Kiory](link= https://forums.bohemia.net/profile/796144-kiory/) and announced on the [text= forums](link= https://forums.bohemia.net/forums/topic/201637-the-bushlurker-monument/), and was later [implemented officially](https://twitter.com/projectargogame/status/867036354617761797).
