|DESCRIPTION=
We have added three Render Worlds that may be used to take pictures or video that needs some keying to add some different scene. They need to be copied into the standard distribution and added as a mod to work correctly in game. Each one of them is of different color, the default one is white while green/blue one is obviously green/blue respectively. You can access it like any other maps, from the mission editor.
