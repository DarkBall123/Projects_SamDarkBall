## For Type

- Source: https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/For_Type
- Revision: 245328
- Updated: 2021-02-10T23:45:38Z

**Description:**
This type handles 'For' cycles.

**Usage of this type:**

for "_var" from :expr: to :expr: [step <expr>] do {..code..};

**Second usage:**

for [":initPhase:",":condition:",":updatePhase:"] do {...code...};

Introduced with Armed Assault, does not refer to Cold War Crisis or Resistance.

Category:Syntactical Helper Types
