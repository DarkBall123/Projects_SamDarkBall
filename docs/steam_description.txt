[i]Crocus is a strike Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) with First‑Person View (FPV) video transmission. Available in two modifications: AT (Anti‑Tank) and AP (Anti‑Personnel).[/i]

[h1][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3147611501] Sania UAV Jammer [/url][/h1]

[h1]Technical Specifications:[/h1]
- Maximum Flight Speed: up to 190 km/h  
- Maximum Range: up to 4 km (up to 6.5 km with a repeater)  
- Flight Time: depends on load and settings

[h1]Additional Features:[/h1]
- Repeater improves signal quality and extends range.  
- The item is called [b]“FPV Signal Booster”[/b] and is visible in Zeus.  
[i]The “FPV Signal Booster” should be placed within 2 km of the player or the drone.[/i]

[h1]For Developers:[/h1]
- The mod operates on the client‑side.  
- The mod is signed.

[h1]Settings:[/h1]  
In Addon Options you can change:
- Max flight range of the drone;  
- The main text in the interface (e.g., “CROCUS”).

[h1]Drone Video Signal Depends On:[/h1]
- Distance to the operator  
- Terrain/obstacles (elevation can block signal)  
- Low flight altitude outside direct line of sight  
- Nearby objects and interference

[h3]Recommended: control the drone with the mouse.[/h3]
